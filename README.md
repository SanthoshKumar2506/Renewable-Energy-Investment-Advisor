# Renewable Energy Investment Advisor

> A decision-support framework for evaluating renewable energy investments and optimizing campus energy capital allocation.

##  Overview
Renewable Energy Investment Advisor is a data-driven decision-support project designed to help Northeastern University evaluate renewable energy investment options. The framework compares energy efficiency, on-site solar, off-site renewable procurement, and electrification strategies using financial, operational, and sustainability metrics.

The goal is to support better capital allocation by identifying investment portfolios that reduce cost, improve payback period, and support long-term carbon reduction goals.

##  Business Problem
Northeastern invests across multiple energy levers, but without a unified framework to compare financial performance, energy impact, and environmental benefits. This creates uncertainty, inconsistent evaluation, and risk of inefficient capital allocation.

##  Solution
The project proposes an Excel-based optimization and decision-support model that evaluates investment alternatives using:

- Cost of Saved Energy (CSE)
- Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE)
- Payback period
- Energy savings
- Emissions reduction
- Scenario analysis
